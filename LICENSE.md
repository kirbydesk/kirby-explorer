## Activation

Each publicly reachable Website is activated once. Activation is free, takes
one click in the panel, and the resulting key does not expire.

When you activate, the Plugin sends the domain of the Website to
`activate.kirbyexplorer.com`. Our server verifies that the domain really
runs the Plugin, and stores the domain, the key and the date. If you
request a key through the form on the website instead, it also stores the
email address you give us, so that we can send the key to you.

We keep this to know where the Plugin is used, and the email address to
answer you. We do not send you unrelated email, and we do not pass the
data on to anyone else. Write to <hello@kirbydesk.com> and we will remove
your entry.

Nothing about your Website's content, its files, its users or their
activity is ever transmitted. After activation the Plugin works entirely
offline; it does not contact us again.

Local Installations are never asked to activate.
